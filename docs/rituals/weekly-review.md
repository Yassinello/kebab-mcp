# Weekly review

**Say:** “weekly review”, “what did I do this week?”, “help me plan next week”.

**What it does:** a ten-minute guided review.

1. **Look back** with `kebab_weekly_recap` (tool calls, skills used, clients, notes added and changed, compared with the week before), your profile's priorities, your calendar when available, and last week's review.
2. **Write** **Done**, **Not done**, **Learned**, **Open loops** and **Next week, top 3**, asking at most two questions to fill gaps.
3. **Save** the review to `Reviews/YYYY-Www.md`, and propose a profile update if your priorities changed (applied only after you agree).

In clients that render cards, the dashboard's 8-week activity chart has a twin: ask for `kebab_status`.

**Needs:** the profile and the vault. Without `kebab_weekly_recap` (older instances), it falls back to `vault_recent` over the last seven days.
